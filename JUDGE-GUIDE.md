# SIR in one minute

Extract the ZIP. With Node.js 22.13+, open **START SIR DEMO.cmd**. Keep its window open; Ctrl+C stops the demonstration.

1. **Tell SIR:** choose the displayed Acorn example and Ask SIR, three times. Review and confirm the understood facts; create the case.
2. **Evidence:** choose Order or agreement; read `fixtures/Acorn_Order_Confirmation.pdf`. Check suggestions, confirm and save.
3. **Complaint:** review the generated wording. Save any edits and continue.
4. **Email:** use `complaints@acorn.example`. Review, explicitly approve and download the unsent email file. Nothing has been sent.
5. **Outcome:** record a fictional reply as unsolicited if no outgoing message was actually sent. Record the fictional refund and explicitly close the case. Open Case history, then reopen through My cases to inspect retained records.

The guide at the top of the app directs each transition. The scripted conversation simulates Alexa+; the local evidence, complaint, approval, export and saved case workflows are implemented. There is no live Alexa or automatic email sending.


Use the left sidebar for stage navigation. An example email address is a placeholder until you enter it; the preview shows the entered recipient. Approval is required before unsent export.
